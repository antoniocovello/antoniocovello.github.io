Stellantis

Electric Powertrain Validation & Charging Test Engineer

October 2023 – January 2025 | Turin, Italy

After obtaining my Master's Degree in Mechanical Engineering at Politecnico di Milano, I decided to turn my passion for the automotive industry into a profession, maintaining a strong interest in sustainability, electric mobility and innovative transportation technologies.

I then started my professional career at Stellantis within the PSS (Propulsion System Service) team, taking on the role of Electric Powertrain Validation & Charging Test Engineer. In this position, I worked on the validation of high-voltage electrical systems, batteries, charging infrastructures and software integration for electric and hybrid vehicles.

Projects and Main Activities

  • EV/EVSE Charging Process Validation Free2move eSolutions Wallbox
    Execution of functional and compatibility tests between different vehicles and charging wallboxes under various operating conditions. Monitoring voltage, current and power values through oscilloscopes, and data analysis to verify the correct operation of the charging process according to SAE J1772.
  • HV Battery Validation for Plug-in Hybrid Vehicles (Jeep Renegade and Jeep Compass)
    Testing and validation activities on high-voltage batteries, focusing on charging, performance, safety, diagnostics and proper electrical system behavior.
  • 332BEV Project (FIAT 500e)
    Validation of software integration within the ECUs of the electric powertrain system, contributing to the development and verification of vehicle functionalities. Software commissioning of BMS (Battery Management System) and EVCU (Electric Vehicle Control Unit). Planning of design and validation activities (DVP), performing tests on ePT components (HV batteries, motor and inverter) and subsequent data analysis. Development and use of CAPL scripts through Vector tools for gateway procedures, leveraging knowledge of CAN and LIN communications.
  • 500e AMPLE Battery Swap
    Validation of the integration of the Ample HV battery into the 500e vehicle system, ensuring safety and correct charging process operation.

Tools & Hard Skills

CDA, DIANalyzer, CANalyzer, Picoscope, CANoe, CAPL, INCA/MDA, MS Office & Excel, Matlab/Simulink
